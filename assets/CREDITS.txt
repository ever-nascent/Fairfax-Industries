Deadlock-style assets from the Deadlock Graphical Library by Lovely (Radiance font, item card style reference):
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1PvXmXNP9MLTSjOmg7fUGChsqaE3hxyh7

Hero chat icons (assets/heroes): deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Map shapes: Natural Earth via the world-atlas package (public domain).

Rank card / urn (assets/fonts, assets/card): Radiance and Retail fonts, the souls icon, the Soul Urn icon and Soul Urn render from deadlock.wiki
(https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve. Level flask shape/colours traced from the in-game HUD.

The Doorman game (assets/doorman): the Doorman's double door (from "Doorman Reference Sheet"), the Baroness Hotel interior
(from "Baroness Hotel Guest Interior") and the Doorman portraits ("The Doorman card", "Doorman Gloat", "Doorman Critical") and his Hotel Guest ability icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Wraith's Table (assets/wraith): her Card Trick cards (cut from "Wraith Reference Sheet"), her portraits ("Wraith card", "Wraith Gloat", "Wraith Critical")
and the Card Trick ability icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Rabbit in the Hat (assets/sinclair): Sinclair's hero-select background ("Sinclair select background", cropped), the rabbit (cut from
"Sinclair Reference Sheet"), his portraits ("Sinclair card", "Sinclair Gloat", "Sinclair Critical") and the Rabbit Hex ability icon
from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Borrowed Time (assets/paradox): Paradox's hero-select background ("Paradox select background", cropped), her portraits
("Paradox card", "Paradox Gloat", "Paradox Critical") and the Pulse Grenade ability icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Yamato's Duel of Stances (assets/yamato): Yamato's portraits ("Yamato card", "Yamato Gloat", "Yamato Critical") and his Power Slash,
Flying Strike and Crimson Slash ability icons from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Silver's Shotgun Roulette (assets/silver): Silver's portraits ("Silver card", "Silver Gloat Portrait", "Silver Critical Health") and the Slam Fire
ability icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve. The shell and charge emojis (white, like the in-game icons) are drawn by assets/silver/make_shells.js.

Holliday's Powder Keg (assets/holliday): Holliday's portraits (card, gloat, critical) and hero-select background (cropped, half size)
from the deadlock-api.com assets API (https://assets.deadlock-api.com), and the Powder Keg ability icon from deadlock.wiki
(https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Bebop's Bombs (assets/bebop): Bebop's portraits ("Bebop card", "Bebop Gloat", "Bebop Critical") and the Sticky Bomb ability icon
from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Rem's Remling Race (assets/rem): Rem's portraits ("Rem card", "Rem Gloat (Familiar)", "Rem Critical (Familar)") and the Lil Helpers ability icon
from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve. The five Remling emojis (remling_<colour>.png) are that icon recoloured.

Deadman Danny's Drop Zone (assets/danny): Deadman Danny's portraits ("Deadman Danny card", "Deadman Danny Gloat", "Deadman Danny Critical")
and the Close The Deal ability icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Shop (assets/shop): hideout.png is the Deadlock minimap Stairs icon (deadlock.wiki "Stairs.png"), redrawn as vector at 128 px.
The /shop banner art in assets/shop/art (curiosity_shop.png = "The Curiosity Shop in-game.png", shop_icon.png = "MinimapShopIcon.png",
rejuv.png = "Mid-Boss.png"; the in-game shop screen "Weapon shop.png" was the style reference)
is from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Lockle item art (assets/lockle/items): the in-game shop item art, downloaded from deadlock-api.com (assets-bucket.deadlock-api.com) the first time each item shows up, game art by Valve. Layout modelled on lockle.app.

Trivia host (assets/dynamo): the Dynamo portraits ("Dynamo card", "Dynamo Gloat", "Dynamo Critical") and his Singularity
ability icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Pocket's Suitcases (assets/pocket): Pocket portraits (synth_card, synth_card_gloat, synth_card_critical) from the deadlock-api.com assets API and the Enchanter's Satchel icon from deadlock.wiki (https://deadlock.wiki), game art by Valve.

Hero Farming (assets/farm): the data files (heroes, abilities, items, npcs .json) are the deadlock.wiki data pages (Data:HeroData.json, AbilityData, ItemCards,
NpcData, GenericData), built by `npm run farm-data`. The icons in assets/farm/icons are deadlock.wiki files: the minimap camp icons (MapEasyCampIcon, MapMediumCampIcon,
MapHardCampIcon), MapSacrificeMachineIcon, Fairfax_crates (a scene picture; the wiki has no Crate icon), Tough_Crate, Sinner's_Sacrifice_machine (the Crate and Sinner's Sacrifice ones are cropped square), Midboss, Guardian_Icon, Walker_Icon, Base_guardians, Shrine_Icon and
Patron_Icon, from https://deadlock.wiki, game art by Valve.
The Golden Statue picture (assets/farm/icons/golden-statue.png, the owl statue) was supplied by Zechariah, game art by Valve.
The Character Unlock Token (assets/farm/icons/unlock-token.png) is deadlock.wiki's "Ultra priority token" file, game art by Valve.
The buff pictures in assets/farm/buffs are the Powerup tome files from deadlock.wiki (Permanent Buff page, https://deadlock.wiki/Permanent_Buff), game art by Valve; which tome is which buff comes from the game's own data (Data:MiscData.json).

assets/farm/icons/lane-york.png, lane-broadway.png, lane-park.png (the lane badges), base-guardian.png, shrine.png, patron.png and
patron-weakened.png: pictures supplied by Zechariah (Oct 3, 2026; Deadlock art, (c) Valve), cropped / background removed here.
assets/farm/icons/trooper.png, trooper-medic.png and trooper-melee.png (the lane troopers): pictures supplied by Zechariah (Oct 3, 2026; Deadlock art,
(c) Valve), background removed here.
assets/farm/ui/ (the weapon / vitality / spirit emblems, stat icons, tier numerals, lock, ability point and lane icons) and assets/farm/items/ (the
HUD icon of every shop item): Deadlock game art, (c) Valve, downloaded Oct 3, 2026 from https://deadlock-api.com (unofficial) for the hero card.

farm/shop/ (the Hero Farming item shop): Deadlock's own shop art (tab backgrounds "catalog_shop_bg_*", item card backs "card_backer_*", tab shape,
tab icons, price glyph) from deadlock-api.com's asset bucket (assets-bucket.deadlock-api.com/assets-api-res/images/shop/catalog), the paths
deadlock-ui (github.com/deadlock-api/deadlock-ui) uses. Downloaded Oct 3, 2026. Deadlock and its art are Valve's.

assets/farm/rivals/ (encounters: each hero's "Gloat" and "Critical" portraits, shown when a rival wins or is beaten): deadlock.wiki files
"<Hero> Gloat" / "<Hero> Critical" (Celeste: "Celeste Critical Health"), game art by Valve. Downloaded Oct 3, 2026 (npm run farm-data).

assets/soulbag/ (the Soul Bag that drops in chat): map.png is deadlock.wiki's "Unsecured Souls" minimap icon, game art by Valve, downloaded
Oct 10, 2026. bag.png is Zechariah's own picture of the in-game Soul Bag with the background colour cut out.
