# Fairfax Industries — Discord server + bot

Deadlock community Discord server for Zechariah's content (TikTok Live + Twitch), fans, and LFG.
`server-plan.md` is the source of truth for decisions. Update it whenever a decision is made.

## How Zechariah likes to work

- One step at a time. End each step with a **Checkpoint:** (no emojis) he can verify in Discord.
- Ask when information is missing; don't guess. Don't reinterpret his wording.
- Search the web for anything about Deadlock that may have changed (heroes, ranks, rules).
- Test changes before handing them over, and say what was and wasn't tested.
- He prefers double-click workflows (`setup.py`, `Start Bot.bat`), not long terminal commands.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|------|------|

Uncaught errors and Discord connection problems are logged, not fatal; every outside `fetch` has a timeout. |

| `test/` | `node --test` (XP, storage, reaction roles, games, casino, Shop, trivia, Lockle, streams). Runs in a temp `DATA_DIR`, never the live `data/`. |

## Bot features

- Member roles (`src/utils/memberRoles.js`): on startup creates Don of the Cursed Apple (owner, given to the server owner), Shrine Keeper (admins), Base Guardian (mods), Citizen of the Cursed Apple (the server unlock, see below). Shown separately, not mentionable. **Gate (Sept 29):** @everyone can't view channels except #welcome, #rules, #roles; Citizen carries View Channel. Picking a Patron in #roles (or holding a staff role) gives Citizen (`src/events/patronUnlock.js`, `isUnlocked` in `memberRoles.js`; startup catches up). Members who joined before the gate keep Citizen; un-picking a Patron doesn't lock again. Shrine Keeper = Administrator; Base Guardian = kick, timeout, voice mute/deafen/move, nicknames, manage messages (set only on creation).
- `/reaction-roles` (Manage Roles): emoji → role menus, single or multi mode. Drafts are saved in `data/` (survive restarts); max 20 options per menu; refuses roles the bot can't give (above its role, managed, @everyone).
- `/lfg mode` (anyone, run inside an `#lfg-<region>` channel): pings the LFG role, posts an embed, and creates a voice lobby in the LFG category. Ranked uses the runner's rank role and locks the lobby to rank ±1 (Eternus: Eternus only). One open lobby per person. The lobby is deleted after N min empty, and its post is deleted with it.
- `/lfg-config` (Manage Channels): `close`, `timeout`, `lobby-category`, `mode`, `show`.
- XP: chat XP (cooldown), optional voice XP, level-ups pay souls. `/rank`, `/urn` (daily souls, streaks), `/leaderboard` (image), a `:souls:` emoji (found by name on startup), `/xp-config` (Manage Server: `chat`, `voice`, `level-up-channel`, `show`). Level-up announcements are off until a channel is set. `/xp` and `/souls` (Manage Server): `add`, `subtract`, `set` (amount, then member) or `reset` (member); level follows XP, no souls paid for levels gained that way.
- `/mini-game game:<name> bet:<souls>` (public) runs the betting games below (one command, `src/commands/games/mini-game.js`; `betCommand` in `casino.js`). No click for 5 min: the game is called off, everything staked comes back, the host gloats with a `TIMEOUT_LINES` line (`expireGames` in `casino.js`, run every 30 s by `src/events/gamesReady.js`). Play Again only works for that game's player. Each game module gives `BUTTON_ID`, `AGAIN_ID`, `games`, `startGame`, `staked`, `timeoutView` (optional `posted(id, message)`, run once the start message is up).
- Guess the Door (public, the Doorman's game): The Baroness Hotel. Min bet 10, max = whole balance. Pick 1 of 3 rooms, the Doorman opens an empty one, Stay or Switch; a win pays 1.5×. Games in progress are saved in `data/doormanGames.json`, so a restart doesn't eat bets. Logic + image: `src/utils/doorman.js`, buttons: `src/components/games.js`, art: `assets/doorman/`.
- Blackjack (public, Wraith's game): Wraith's Table. Hit / Stand / Double Down / Split, one Joker in the deck (whoever draws it wins the hand). Win 2×, blackjack 3:2. Games in progress saved in `data/blackjackGames.json`. Logic + image: `src/utils/blackjack.js`, buttons: `src/components/games.js`, art: `assets/wraith/`.
- Rabbit in the Hat (public, Sinclair's game): shuffle GIF, pick Hat 1–3, a win pays 2.5×. Games in progress saved in `data/hatGames.json`. Logic + images: `src/utils/sinclair.js` (GIFs via the `gifenc` package), buttons: `src/components/games.js`, art: `assets/sinclair/`.
- Borrowed Time (public, Paradox's game): higher or lower on a clock, each right call multiplies the prize, Cash Out any time. Games in progress saved in `data/clockGames.json`. Logic + image: `src/utils/paradox.js`, buttons: `src/components/games.js`, art: `assets/paradox/`.
- Bebop's Bombs (public, Bebop's game, casino Mines): 20 emoji-button tiles (4×5) + Cash Out, 4 sticky bombs; each safe tile raises the prize (95% of fair odds), a bomb loses. Games in progress saved in `data/minesGames.json`. Logic + board: `src/utils/bebop.js` (no picture; `gameMessage` takes `rows` of emoji buttons), art: `assets/bebop/`, `:sticky_bomb:` emoji found on startup (`gamesReady.js`).
- Powder Keg (public, Holliday's game, casino Crash): the start message is a 20 s GIF (8 fps, ~1.8 MB, drawn per bet, the last 20 bets cached) of a plain white fuse thrown in one big lasso loop (it carries on from the Powder Keg icon's own fuse) burning at a steady pace, a comic flame in the explosion's style at the burning end, while the multiplier counts up 1× → 10× (×2 at 6 s) with the souls a Cash Out would pay above it; the bot's clock is what counts: Cash Out pays bet × the multiplier at the click, the keg blows on a timer set when the message goes up (`posted`); the clock starts `GIF_DELAY_MS` (1 s) after posting, about when the GIF appears on screen. Crash point: the chance it lasts past m is 95% / m (5% blow at 1.00×), always at the 10× cap. Cash-out picture: fuse snuffed with smoke, "would have blown at"; boom picture: the "Shatter" explosion (keg icon in pieces over a halftone fireball). Games in `data/kegGames.json`; leftovers from a restart (timer lost) are refunded at startup. Logic + images: `src/utils/holliday.js`, art: `assets/holliday/`.
- Duel of Stances (public, Yamato's game, rock paper scissors; `PVP = true` in the module, so `/mini-game` accepts `opponent:`): Power Slash beats Flying Strike beats Crimson Slash beats Power Slash, stances are buttons with `:power_slash:` / `:flying_strike:` / `:crimson_slash:` emojis (found on startup). No opponent: vs Yamato, he picks at the start, win 1.85×, tie refunds. With an opponent: pings them, Accept (they stake the same) / Decline, secret picks (the button answers with a private reply, `{ private, update }` in `gameButtons`), winner takes the pot, tie refunds both, Rematch for either duelist (`duel_again:<bet>:<a>:<b>`). A game module can give `refunds(game)` when more than one member staked (`expireGames`). Games in `data/duelGames.json`. Logic: `src/utils/yamato.js`, art: `assets/yamato/`, test `test/yamato.test.js`.
- Shotgun Roulette (public, Silver's game, Buckshot Roulette core loop, no items yet; `PVP = true`): shotgun with 2-8 shells (1-3 live, counts shown, order hidden), 3 charges each, Shoot the other or Shoot Yourself (blank on yourself = shoot again, live on yourself = turn passes), empty chamber reloads, last one standing wins. No opponent: vs Silver, you shoot first, she answers automatically in the same message (she shoots herself only when no live shell is left; any riskier rule lost to always-shooting-her in simulation), win 1.85x. With `opponent:`: Accept / Decline, winner takes the pot, Rematch. Games in `data/rouletteGames.json`. Logic: `src/utils/silver.js`, art: `assets/silver/`, `:shell_live:` / `:shell_blank:` / `:charge:` / `:charge_lost:` emojis (white like the in-game icons (hearts are outlines), drawn by `assets/silver/make_shells.js`, found on startup), test `test/silver.test.js`.
- Remling Race (public, Rem's game, a horse race; solo): 5 Remlings (`:remling_red:` ... `:remling_pink:`, found on startup), pick a lane (buttons show the odds), then the message is edited every 2 s as they run **left to right, finish flag on the right**, as emoji + dash text lanes, `> **1.** - - 🔴 - - 🏁` (no picture). Chances 36/26/20/11/7 % dealt to the lanes at random, a win pays 95% / chance. The winner is drawn first and a random race is re-run until they win it (exact odds). Games in `data/remlingGames.json`; a restart mid-race refunds at startup (`gamesReady.js`). Logic: `src/utils/rem.js`, art: `assets/rem/`, test `test/rem.test.js`.
- Deal or No Deal (public, Pocket's game; solo): 20 suitcases as a 4x5 button grid, pick one to keep, open 18 in rounds (5,4,3,3,2,1) with a Deal / No Deal offer after each, then Keep or Swap. Cases hold 0.05x-6x the bet, average 0.95x; offers 55-94% of the remaining average. Games in `data/pocketGames.json`. Logic: `src/utils/pocket.js`, art: `assets/pocket/`, test `test/pocket.test.js`.
- `/trivia mode:<Heroes|Items>` (public message, solo, **hosted by Dynamo**: author "Dynamo's Classroom" with his Singularity icon, his portrait as thumbnail, gloat when you're right, injured when wrong or time's up (an encouraging teacher: lines never mock), one of his `RIGHT_LINES` / `WRONG_LINES` / `TIMEOUT_LINES` in the footer; art in `assets/dynamo/`; the answer's hero shows as its emoji in the text): only the runner answers, one guess, no cooldown. Right answers pay by streak (25, 30 ... 70 at 10 in a row; wrong or time's up resets), only 10 paid right answers per UTC day (`triviaAnswer` in `xp.js`); a 10-emoji streak bar (`:streak_*:` emojis drawn by the server's emojis, found on startup). Embed turns green (right) or red (wrong / time's up) when the round ends. No mode = any question. Item questions: cost, tier, fill in a % stat, blurred item (5-item dropdown), type the item's name (pop-up), what X upgrades into (dropdown, ends on a build graph drawn by `upgradeGraph`); the dropdown/typed ones get 30 s. Questions come from `assets/trivia/trivia.json` + `items.json` (build/refresh both with `setup.py trivia`). Logic: `src/utils/trivia.js`, buttons: `src/components/trivia.js`. Rounds live in memory (20 s, no bets).
- `/lockle daily|practice`: guess the hero from a real Ascendant+ ranked build (deadlock-api.com match data, unofficial), 6 guesses from hero dropdowns, 2 more items per wrong guess, inline fields Match Time (when the last shown item was bought), Rank, Souls Spent (shop prices added up), souls on guess 6. Picture drawn like lockle.app: cream panel, in-game shop item art (saved to `assets/lockle/items/` on first use), 6 guess slots with hero icons, wrong ones faded with a red outline, Retail font (his call; it draws `:` and `/` as a souls glyph, so the caption says "Guess 1 of 6"). Daily: same build for everyone (midnight UTC), private, pays 200/170/140/110/80/50, saved in `data/lockle.json`. Practice: public, no souls, in memory. `src/utils/lockle.js`, `src/components/lockle.js`, test `test/lockle.test.js`.
- Go-live alerts, Twitch + TikTok (`src/utils/streams.js`, `src/events/streamsReady.js`): checks every minute for `TWITCH_LOGIN` (`charmedvt`) and `TIKTOK_LOGIN` (`charmed.dl`), both in `config.js`; posts `@everyone` + an embed (Twitch purple with stream thumbnail, TikTok pink with profile picture) in #streams-and-uploads (`STREAM_CHANNEL_ID` in `config.js`). One ping per stream; a reconnect within 10 min doesn't ping again; the very first check of a platform only remembers, never pings (so switching one on mid-stream is quiet). Last streams saved in `data/twitch.json` (`last` = Twitch, `lastTiktok`; file name predates TikTok). Twitch is off until `setup.py twitch` saves the keys. TikTok has no official API: it reads the endpoint tiktok.com's live page uses (unofficial, may break). `/stream-config description` (Manage Server) opens a box to set the text under the stream title for both (empty = none) and shows a private preview.
- Censor (`src/utils/censor.js`, `src/events/censor.js`, `/auto-mod censor add|remove|list` (`src/commands/admin/auto-mod.js`, room for more auto-mod groups), Manage Server; phrases in `data/censor.json`): a message (or edit) containing a censored phrase as a whole word (case/accent-insensitive; "ass" doesn't hit "class", so variants like "fucking" need their own entry) is deleted and the author gets a `WARN_LINES` reply in the Shopkeeper's voice, which deletes itself after 15 s and never repeats the phrase. The bot's own actions are skipped, except roles members pick from its menus (role change reason starts with "Self-picked").

Item pages are drawn by `renderItemPage` in `shopBanner.js`. Hero Cards section: Two dropdowns with hero emojis; picking one redraws the embed image as that hero's card with the runner's stats.
